# AI-Enhanced EASM (2025)

AI fundamentally changes how EASM works.

### 🔹 AI in Asset Discovery
- LLMs automate recon queries (e.g., ask GPT to enumerate subdomains from multiple APIs).
- AI handles **data merging, deduplication, validation**.

### 🔹 AI in Threat Intel
- NLP to scan forums, ransomware blogs, Telegram groups.
- Summarize chatter → flag relevant to your org.

### 🔹 AI in Analysis
- AI models detect anomalies in DNS changes, SSL updates, login pages.
- Automated **phishing detection** on domains.

### 🔹 AI in Action
- Instead of SOC Tier-1 manual triage:
  - AI filters false positives.
  - Escalates only real threats.
  - Auto-generates reports for SOC analysts.

✅ AI = EASM at machine scale, freeing analysts to focus on decision-making.
