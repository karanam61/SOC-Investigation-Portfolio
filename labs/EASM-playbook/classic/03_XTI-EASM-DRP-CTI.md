# Extended Threat Intelligence (XTI): EASM + DRP + CTI

XTI combines 3 intelligence layers:

---

## 1. External Attack Surface Management (EASM)
- Detect and track all internet-facing assets.
- Alerts:
  - New asset detected
  - Domain/DNS changes
  - Expired SSL
  - Subdomain takeover
  - Vulnerability detected

## 2. Digital Risk Protection (DRP)
- Protect brand reputation and assets.
- Alerts:
  - Phishing domains
  - Rogue mobile apps
  - Impersonating social media
  - Dark web mentions
  - Stolen credit cards
  - Botnets using org infra
  - Data leaks in repos/buckets

## 3. Cyber Threat Intelligence (CTI)
- Broad intelligence on global campaigns, ransomware groups, IOCs.
- Integrated into SIEM/SOAR/EDR tools for automation.

---
✅ **Output**: Together, EASM + DRP + CTI = **XTI**, a unified visibility and defense model.
